Hi Everyone!

Wow. Six months. Not counting the MegaMek-only Core Rules release we did in August, this is one of the longest development cycles the programs have ever had. It needed to be, because this is the biggest release we've ever done.

As you can see by the numbers below, we have pushed an unbelievable amount of code into the programs. Since 0.51.0 shipped on June 6, MegaMek, MegaMekLab and MekHQ have taken in more than 7,000 commits and about a million new lines, and roughly a third of those lines are unit tests.

| **Total** | **7,239** | **999,538** | **185,706** | **340,488** |

So let's say this right up front. There is no way on this planet that a changelog can cover everything in this release. The Feature Highlights from the past six weeks cover the big features. These notes cover the rest, grouped by theme. The full changelog at the bottom lists every pull request, and we're doing a better job of labelling each one, so you can tell whether it adds something new, fixes something, or does something else.

**How we got here**

We could build a release this size because, for a good chunk of this summer, we had more developers working on the projects than ever before. Things have slowed down since.

You've heard us talk about tech debt. If you haven't, here's what it means. Tech debt is the cost of old shortcuts. Code that was written in a hurry to get a feature out, or written years ago for a much smaller program, has to be worked around every time we change something near it. Think of a house where the wiring was added one room at a time. Every new light switch takes longer to put in, and it's more likely to blow a fuse somewhere else. Paying down tech debt is the rewiring. You don't see it, but everything after it gets faster and safer to build.

With all the code we've added, changed or touched in the last six months, we've made sizable dents in that debt, and it's finally going to start paying dividends. New features will get easier to build, and we can catch up on things we haven't been able to implement. We're also looking at improvements to the MegaMek interface that line up with the interface changes already made in MekHQ. MegaMekLab will follow, but for the next few cycles the focus stays on MegaMek and MekHQ.

So long-time players will open this release and, in some ways, think nothing has changed. In other ways, everything will feel different.

<div class="corerules-callout" markdown="1">
<p class="corerules-callout-title">A word from the team</p>

That leads into something important, and we want to take a moment for it.

We are a volunteer team. Looking at what the programs already do, and what rules still need to be implemented, we've realized we need a slightly different approach from this release on. We've always tried to fit everyone's needs within what the programs can do. We're not going to stop taking requests, but we are going to be more selective about which ones we build. We can't implement something just because one player asks for it on the tracker or on Discord. It needs to be a need shared by a larger group of players, and the developers need to see its importance too. That doesn't mean it will never happen. It means being honest about expectations. We are the guardians of these projects, and we have to move them forward in the way we think is right.

There's one more thing we need to call out, because it's a pattern we've seen more of this year. People are forgetting that most of our developers work full-time jobs. They do this for fun, as a hobby, and for the love of the community. Some of the comments and reactions aimed at MegaMek and the other projects have been very disheartening. Please remember that we're more than the handles you see on the screen. We're people too. We have lost developers in the past because of how the community reacted to decisions we made, and that has been true for as long as the projects have existed.

So we ask for a little understanding, a little kindness, and a little trust. We love this game and this universe every bit as much as you do. But in the end, we have to make decisions based on what's right for the programs' long-term success, and for the people who volunteer to code them.
</div>

### 2. A New Release Rhythm: Core Releases and Experimental Releases

**Draft (from HammerGS's dictation):**

Over MegaMek's lifetime, our approach to releases has changed again and again.

Way back, we had a **stable** release and a **development** release. It wasn't uncommon to sit on a stable release for a couple of years while the development releases moved on. Those ranged from really good releases that honestly should have been called stable, to really unstable ones that should never have been released at all.

We then moved to **milestones**. A milestone was simply a release we marked after the fact. If we felt a development release was a good one, we declared it a milestone, and development releases kept moving forward the same way as before.

The Launcher changes things for us, and that's why we're now adopting two streams: **Core releases** and **Experimental releases**. The Launcher post's [A New Model of Releases](https://megamek.org/2026/11/02/Feature-Highlight-The-MegaMek-Launcher.html#a-new-model-of-releases) section covers how often each one ships. Here's what they mean for you.

**Core releases are releases we feel we can stand on.** 0.51.01 is our first one, and it's where almost everyone should be.

**Experimental releases are something you opt into,** and we need to say this in very clear language. There is really only one reason to be on Experimental: you want to be a playtester, one of our Test Pilots. If you opt in, then when you come to Discord or open a bug report, we're going to treat it as playtesting. Experimental builds change often, and a lot of things in them will be broken.

<div class="corerules-callout release-warning" markdown="1">
<p class="corerules-callout-title">Don't opt into Experimental unless you mean it</p>

Experimental is beyond the bleeding edge. Only opt in if you're also fully prepared to report the bugs and issues you find. If you just want to play, stay on Core.
</div>

That's also why all three programs now have a bright red **Report a Bug** button. More on that in [Reporting Bugs Has Changed](#7-reporting-bugs-has-changed) below.

<div class="corerules-callout" markdown="1">

- **You can tell which one you're running.** Experimental builds have a four-part version number (0.51.01.x), and the build's channel is stamped into it.

#### 4a. Core Rules and Walk-on Deployment

Back in August we did something we'd never done before: a MegaMek-only release. [0.51.00.1, the Core Rules Release](https://megamek.org/2026/08/14/MegaMek-Core-Rules-Release-0.51.00.1.html) brought MegaMek in line with Catalyst's *BattleTech Core Rulebook*, and [Core Rules Phase One](https://megamek.org/announcements/development/2026/08/11/Core-Rules-Phase-One-and-a-MegaMek-Only-Release.html) explains why we did it that way. If you play MegaMekLab or MekHQ, this is the first time all of that work reaches you.

Since we launched the Core Rules, we've had a lot of very positive feedback on how they play. It also helped that we'd already run the playtests in MegaMek. We've kept implementing, refining and fixing the Core Rules since then, and we're happy to announce that we've brought in one of the most wanted features in MegaMek: **walk-on deployment**.

It's always on when you play under the Core Rules, and MegaMek now defaults to the Core Rules. Playing Total Warfare? Turn on the **Walk-on Deployment** game option and you can use it there too.

Here's what changes. Your units don't appear on the map in a separate Deployment phase any more. They march on from your map edge during the Movement phase, and deploying is part of their move. [8812]

- **How it works.** In the Movement phase, your first click picks the edge hex the unit enters on. Your second click plots its move from there. Press **Escape** to clear both and pick a different hex. Press **Move**, and the unit enters and moves in one go. For example, your Marauder MAD-3R enters on the north edge, pays for the terrain it steps onto, and spends the rest of its MP walking into the woods.
- **The edge is the edge.** Deployment zones are one hex deep: the map edge itself. The lobby's player settings tell you so.
- **Not every unit walks on.** Units set to deploy **Any** or **Center**, and units with their deployment round set to **Pre-game**, are placed the old way before the game starts. Immobile units skip movement and initiative entirely.
- **It works with the rest of the game.** Units carrying troops, vehicles pulling trailers (the train turns to fit if it has to), individual initiative, jumping, aerospace on space maps, and fighters coming back after flying off all walk on properly. The movement envelope shows the right reach for a unit that hasn't entered yet. [9120, 9131, 9202, 9016, 9563]
- **The bots walk on too.** We've trained both Princess and CASPAR to use it ([Princess and CASPAR, Round Two](https://megamek.org/2026/11/15/Feature-Highlight-Princess-and-CASPAR-Round-Two.html), "Smarter Decisions"). [9146, 9545, 9608, 9676]

<div class="corerules-callout release-warning" markdown="1">
<p class="corerules-callout-title">Please report anything odd</p>

Walk-on deployment has had extensive testing, but it's also been one of the most complex and challenging things we've ever had to implement. Roundtop has done a spectacular job with it. If you're playing with walk-on deployment, and under the Core Rules you are, please report any oddities or unusual behaviour with Report a Bug.
</div>

The rest of the Core Rules work since August:

- **Autocannon critical hits** now work for Meks, vehicles and aerospace units.
- **Shields, lances and claws** follow the Core Rules.
- **Indirect fire modifiers**, **kicks, punches and water PSRs**, **road elevation costs** and the **DFA push direction** were all updated.
- **Special armor** rounds the Core Rules way.
- **An immobile unit no longer costs you a move.**
- **Errata:** we applied Core Rules Errata 0.1 BV for alternate munitions, the Light and Heavy Plasma Rifle BV, and the escalation rules for MASC, Blue Shield and Supercharger.
- **Rulebook references** now show throughout ammo, equipment and artillery, so you can look up where a rule comes from.

When we introduced the Core Rules, we asked for extra eyes on a few things that sit in very old code ([Where We'd Like Eyes](https://megamek.org/2026/08/14/MegaMek-Core-Rules-Release-0.51.00.1.html#where-wed-like-eyes)): superheavy Mek ammo and explosive weapon crits, underwater energy weapons, line of sight to submerged Meks, and seatbelt Piloting Skill Rolls. So far we haven't had much feedback on those, and no issues have come in about them. We're going to take that as a sign they're working correctly. If you find otherwise, let us know with Report a Bug.

#### 4b. New

Anything marked **alpha**, **beta** or **experimental** is here early: it works, but expect rough edges, and please tell us what you find.

##### The Big Ones
We wrote a Feature Highlight for each of these. We're not going to repeat them here, so if one sounds like your game, click through.
- **[Objectives and Victory Points](https://megamek.org/2026/10/23/Feature-Highlight-Objectives-and-Victory-Points.html) (alpha).** Games can be about more than killing everything on the map now. You place objective points in a new Victory Setup phase, and each one scores in one of six ways:
  - **Standard** (Core Rulebook): every End Phase, you score 1 victory point if you hold one of your own points and one of the enemy's, or 2 if you hold them all.
  - **Raid** (Core Rulebook): whoever holds the point when the game ends scores it.
  - **Scan** (Core Rulebook): the point isn't held, it's read. Send a scout to scan it. Depending on the setup, you're paid when the scan succeeds, paid but lose it if the scout dies, or paid only once the scout gets home.
  - **Hold** (MegaMek): the first side to hold the point for a set number of turns secures it.
  - **Defend** (MegaMek): the point belongs to its owner. Every turn an enemy unit stands in the zone, the owner's grip slips, and at zero the point falls.
  - **Capture** (MegaMek): attackers fill a progress meter by controlling the zone, and the owner pushes it back by retaking it.

  The bots don't play objectives yet.
- **[Boots on the Ground](https://megamek.org/2026/10/30/Feature-Highlight-Boots-on-the-Ground.html).** A big cycle for infantry. Troops fight room to room inside buildings, Advanced Buildings get crews and turrets, and infantry weapons got a full data pass. It also covers troops riding VTOLs and WiGEs, tainted and toxic atmospheres, and armor kits and sidearms for your crews.
- **[Princess and CASPAR, Round Two](https://megamek.org/2026/11/15/Feature-Highlight-Princess-and-CASPAR-Round-Two.html).** CASPAR, our new bot, is now yours to play against (**experimental**). You also get the Bot Control Panel, unit orders (**alpha**), artillery fire missions, and bots that understand honor and rules of engagement. About 30 bot fixes are in there too, including the bots' artillery, so we won't list them again below.
- **[The Command Generator](https://megamek.org/2026/11/21/Feature-Highlight-The-Command-Generator.html).** Mostly a MekHQ post, but it also covers the MegaMek **Force Generator**: Add to Game, formations that know which faction they belong to, and Heroic and Legendary skill levels.

##### Game Master Tools (beta)
In 0.51.00.1 we rebuilt Game Master mode around the Edit Damage console ([Core Rules Phase One](https://megamek.org/announcements/development/2026/08/11/Core-Rules-Phase-One-and-a-MegaMek-Only-Release.html), "Game Master mode, rebuilt"). This release goes further: the Game Master now controls the battlefield itself, not just the units on it. [What's Coming](https://megamek.org/2026/10/16/0.51.01-what's-coming.html) has videos of it in action. To become the Game Master, request the role from the **Commands** menu, and the other players vote on it.
- **Reshape the battlefield mid-game.** Right-click a hex for **Change Terrain** or **Modify Building** (both still marked **alpha** in the menus). They work like a paint brush: set what you want, then paint one hex or seven at a time. Flood a valley, burn the woods down to light, raise a hill, put up a warehouse, or knock one flat. MegaMek refuses anything illegal and tells you why, and **Undo** takes back the last change. [8803]
- **Bring a player into a game in progress.** **Commands → Set Up Player** does it in one dialog: pick a team, choose the edge they come in from, then **Apply and Reinforce** from a MUL file or the unit generator. Mid-game reinforcements now live under the Game Master menu, so players can't hand themselves a fresh lance any more. [8813, 9403]
- **Hand out forces from the lobby.** Add Unit, Create Random Army, the Force Generator and Load MUL can all put units on *any* player's force. Before, they quietly landed on yours. [8808, 8861]
- **A deeper Edit Damage console.** Flip any unit's equipment on or off, or change its mode, from ECM and C3 to MASC and AMS. Charge Bombast Lasers and PPC Capacitors. Mark weapons Jammed or Fired, mark Mek locations Breached or Blown Off, and **Explode** explosive equipment (`/explode`). Set per-unit ejection, add a this-round **Target Modifier** (`/targetMod`), and **Restore Unit** now brings back blown-off limbs. [8770, 9027]
- **Give orders to bot units.** Tell a bot unit to withdraw now, fight to the death, or follow its bot's usual rules. With CASPAR, you can also give enemy lances routes and roles. [9076]
- **Run the objectives.** Place, edit and remove victory points mid-game, and write what a scan reveals.

##### Rules
- **Recon Cameras** (TO:AUE p.150).
  - A ground unit with one spots one enemy a turn for LRM indirect fire, rolled like TAG but with no spotting penalty.
  - A fighter at altitude 5 to 10 can spot for LRMs and artillery, or reveal hidden units it flies over.
  - It also comes as a bomb pod, and the bots know how to use it. [9090]
- **Oblique Artilleryman reaches further.** The pilot ability (CamOps p.78) now gives its extra 10% artillery range. An Inner Sphere Arrow IV reaches 150 hexes instead of 136. [8730]
- **Choose the sensor a unit starts on.** Units no longer always start the battle on radar. [8950]
  - Rank your preferred sensors once in Client Settings.
  - Or pick a starting sensor for a unit in the lobby.
  - Tick **Remember for this unit type** and every unit of that design starts on it, in MekHQ too.
- **Switch Artemis and Apollo off.** Artemis IV, Artemis V, Proto Artemis and the Apollo fire control system have On and Off modes, like ECM. An MRM 20 with Apollo switched off takes the usual +1 to-hit and rolls clusters unmodified. [8950]
- **The Virtual Reality Piloting Pod** cockpit (IO:AE p.63) plays by its rules. [9009]
  - The pilot takes -1 gunnery and -2 piloting.
  - Hostile ECM, EMI and similar effects blind the pilot, unless friendly ECCM holds the interference off.
  - It gets the same protection as a torso-mounted cockpit.
- **Avoiding Shutdown is its own option.** Turning on the expanded heat scale used to switch on the TacOps Avoiding Shutdown rule too. Now you can use either one without the other, under either ruleset. [9082]

##### Around the Table
- **Ping a player.** [9501]

  - In a game, use **Commands > Ping**. In the lobby, right-click a player's row.

- **Which bot is that?** Princess and CASPAR keep the same name when you switch them, so the lobby chat now says which one you got. [9538]

- **Damage on the minimap.** Each unit gets a coloured dot: green for light damage, yellow for moderate, red for heavy, black for crippled. It follows your **Show damage level** setting. [9546]

#### 4d. Bug Fixes

We said it at the top, and we'll say it again: there's no way to list every fix in this release. So here are the ones you're most likely to have run into, grouped by what you'd be doing at the time. For the full list, see **Help > Release Notes** in MegaMek, or the full changelog at the bottom of this post. Bot fixes are all in the [Princess and CASPAR highlight](https://megamek.org/2026/11/15/Feature-Highlight-Princess-and-CASPAR-Round-Two.html).

##### Game Master
- **Skip Current Turn skips any turn.** It used to work only on a disconnected player's turn, so it almost always said "skip failed". Other players can still only skip a stuck turn. [9401]
- **Start a Fire and Start a Firestorm work with the default options.** They used to need the TacOps Start Fires option, which is really about attacks starting fires. [9402]
- **The right colours on the minimap.** A Game Master with no units, or an observer watching bots play, now sees each side in its lobby colour instead of everything in enemy red. [9537, 9534]

##### Infantry
We fixed a lot of infantry bugs this cycle, and [Boots on the Ground](https://megamek.org/2026/10/30/Feature-Highlight-Boots-on-the-Ground.html) covers most of them. These are the ones it didn't mention:
- **Fighter missiles against infantry.** They now use the infantry damage table. An SRM 6 against a platoon in the open kills 6 troopers, not 16. [9260]
- **iATM IMP missiles** fired at an airborne fighter now do damage and apply their IMP effects. Before, they did nothing. [9260]
- **Battle armor munitions.** Autoconfig and Randomize only load legal rounds for battle armor (no more BattleMek rounds such as Acid or Tandem-Charge), and bins keep their shot count. Clan SRM, LRM and ATM bins now actually change when you autoconfig. [9236]
- **Battle armor with turrets** that failed to load now loads. [9553]
- **Battle armor costs.** The Myomer Booster's weight and cost are fixed, and AP, modular and turret weapon mounts cost the right amount. [9207]
- **Aimed shots** at a tank or battle armor squad in partial cover no longer crash the game. [8854]
- **Mechanized infantry** (hover, wheeled or tracked) can have 5 squads per platoon, not 4. [9634]
- **Conventional infantry with a Support PPC** are checked for validity and limited to 2 MP. [9423]

##### Buildings
- **Burning buildings tell you what's happening.** Each turn the report shows how much Construction Factor the fire took and what's left, and the hex comes down the moment it reaches 0. [8809]
- **Point-blank shots from hidden turrets.** A revealed hidden unit can rotate its turret before firing, and the shot goes off. [8961]
- **Semi-Guided missiles** fired at a building or hex no longer freeze the game. [8731]
- **Bridge heights.** Standing on a bridge or fuel tank, the Choose Target dialog shows the real height instead of -2,147,483,648. [9560]

##### VTOLs, WiGEs and Hovercraft
WiGEs got a top-to-bottom pass against Total Warfare and TO:AUE. [What's Coming](https://megamek.org/2026/10/16/0.51.01-what's-coming.html) gave it one line, so here's the full list.
- **No free cliff climbing.** An airborne WiGE can only enter a hex one level above the ground it's flying over (TW p.55). An AirMek at elevation 1 used to hop up a level 6 cliff for 1 MP. [9116]
- **No extra MP going downhill.** WiGEs start each turn following the terrain instead of holding altitude, so a glider ProtoMek flying 4 hexes down a slope pays 4 MP, not 12. Keep Elevation is still there if you want it. [9117]
- **Low bridges.** An airborne WiGE can cross a low bridge from any direction, for +2 MP on that hex. [9118]
- **Sideslips** follow TW pp.67–68. Crash damage counts every hex moved that turn, and the slip distance is the margin of failure. [9123]
- **WiGE jumps** follow TO:AUE p.162. The jump ends one level above where the WiGE comes down, it can't jump into woods, and it can't jump from the ground. [9155]
- **Landing.** A WiGE can only land safely in a clear, paved or water hex. Anywhere else is a crash. [9141]
- **Crashing.** A WiGE forced down by damage follows WiGE rules, not VTOL rules. Over a lake, it makes a Driving roll and lands on the water instead of sinking, and any infantry aboard roll to survive. [9142]
- **Grounded WiGEs** follow hover terrain rules, so no magma or ultra-rough. Support WiGEs now follow the same rules as combat WiGEs. [9139]
- **Weak buildings.** A WiGE flying over one brings down the whole hex, not just the top floor. Each WiGE is checked on its own tonnage. [9137]
- **Bots take off again** with a grounded WiGE, instead of crawling 1 hex a turn. [9143]
- **Hovercraft and submerged units.** A hovercraft on water can't see or shoot units on the bottom, and they can't see it. [9136]
- **No flying in a vacuum.** Hovercraft, WiGEs and VTOLs can't operate on a vacuum map, even with Environmental Sealing. [8832]
- **Flak** keeps its -2 against a VTOL, WiGE or AirMek that flew this turn and then landed. [9134]
- **Hidden units.** A VTOL or WiGE that deploys in the air can't deploy hidden. [9135]
- **Blowing sand** no longer damages VTOLs waiting to enter the map. [9393]

  - AAA, LAA and ASEW missiles reach Medium-Capital range, and AS missiles reach Long-Capital. LAA and ASEW can be loaded.

##### Artillery and Ordnance
- **Artillery in flight and saves.** A game saved with FASCAM or Thunder-Vibrabomb in the air no longer crashes when the round lands. A game with a round still in flight after its firer left can be loaded again. [9295, 8801]

##### Movement, Terrain and Physical Attacks

  - With Climbing on and Leaping off, a Mek can't step down 3 or more levels as an ordinary walk.

- **Hidden friends.** A Mek can't end its move on a hidden Mek from its own side. [9588]

#### 6a. Before You Upgrade Your Campaign

**Draft (a mix of HammerGS's and Illiani's voices):**

<div class="corerules-callout release-warning" markdown="1">
<p class="corerules-callout-title">Existing MekHQ campaigns</p>

0.51.01 will happily load your older campaigns, but when it does, **every open contract and mission is closed automatically.** Outstanding payments are collected, POWs are ransomed, and the contract counts as a complete success. You don't need to do anything special. It just happens.

We're going to be upfront with you, though. 0.51.01 is a whole new beast, and it deserves to be met on its own terms. If there was ever a time to start a new campaign, it's now. If you do carry a campaign forward, please switch it to one of the **default presets** when you upgrade.

More in [The Contract Overhaul Project](https://megamek.org/2026/10/20/Feature-Highlight-The-Contract-Overhaul-Project.html) and [Balancing Gameplay and Economics](https://megamek.org/2026/11/07/Feature-Highlight-Balancing-Gameplay-and-Economics.html).
</div>

If you're carrying a campaign forward anyway, here's what will look different the first time you open it.

- **Your admin department just got bigger.** Admin/Logistics, Admin/Transport and the rest are gone. There are now just Administrators, and every one of them can do every admin job. That may leave you with far more admins than you need. The paperwork has never been better staffed.
- **Every skill can have a Natural Aptitude now,** not just combat skills. That brings us fully in line with A Time of War. Existing characters are grandfathered in.
- **New skills arrive: Piloting/ProtoMek and the tech specializations.** Existing characters are grandfathered in here too. These mostly lay the groundwork for the character builder coming in 0.60.00. Going forward, the idea is that you specialize your techs instead of making every one of them an omni-tech.
- **Training formations have been replaced completely.** The old ones were buggy, so we started over. Check the in-game Glossary for how the new ones work.
- **StratCon plays differently.** Every combat role works differently now, morale has been reworked, and based on your feedback, "required combat elements" is gone. The Glossary has the details on all three.
- **Owed a Veterancy award?** If a character earned one in 0.51.00 and never got it, it arrives the next time they improve a skill.
- **Don't like the random personality descriptions?** A new campaign option hides them, either completely or down to just the traits ("Aggressive, Passionate"). We're rewriting them all, but that didn't make it into this release.

#### 6c. New Features

##### Campaign & Contracts
- **Contract Overhaul Project:** we spent the entire 0.51.01 development cycle on a titanic effort to rewrite every aspect of the contract experience. Expect new mechanics, an all-new UI and some surprises. [Read the Contract Overhaul highlight →](https://megamek.org/2026/10/20/Feature-Highlight-The-Contract-Overhaul-Project.html)
- **Command Generator:** the aging Company Generator has been replaced by a brand-new Command Generator with full support for combined arms. [Read the Command Generator highlight →](https://megamek.org/2026/11/21/Feature-Highlight-The-Command-Generator.html)
- **New faction standing Ultimatums.** You can now take a campaign from the dawn of the Star League Civil War all the way to the Exodus and the birth of the Clans.
- **The Bandit Caste** is now a playable faction.
- **Planet-to-planet travel:** you can now travel between planets on the interstellar map, not just between systems.

##### Personnel
- **Cheating death:** a character with Edge (spent or unspent) who would die instead permanently spends a point of Edge to survive. A new option gives every founder one point of Edge.
- **Chassis Familiarity:** characters build up skill at piloting or repairing specific chassis.
- **Personal equipment:** you can now equip personnel with skill-improving toolkits and armor, which makes swapping armor kits for infantry quick. See [Boots on the Ground → In Your Campaign](https://megamek.org/2026/10/30/Feature-Highlight-Boots-on-the-Ground.html). Issuing sidearms will come in 0.60.00. ✎ *MegaMek already supports crew sidearms in 0.51.01, so make clear that only issuing them from MekHQ is still to come.*
- **Romance and reproduction:** same-sex couples can now adopt children. Single fathers are also supported, mirroring single mothers: a character takes custody of a child born outside the campaign. ✎ *Illiani's original gave specific examples ("a local sex worker or a one-night fling"). I've softened that for the public post. Restore it if you'd prefer.*

##### Medical
- **Doctors with MedTech/Any** reduce a patient's injury penalty, so healing is more reliable.
- **Doctors with Tech/Cybernetics** reduce a patient's implant penalty, so cybernetically enhanced characters heal more reliably.

##### Units & Logistics
- **Fabrication:** techs can make replacement parts and ammo following the CamOps rules. This represents a tech in a machine shop making a one-off custom replacement, not a factory production line.
- **Unit history:** units now keep an automatic log, like the personal logs characters already have.

##### Roleplay & Reference
- **Roleplay engine and journal:** use the oracle system to drive roleplay in your campaign, and the in-game journal to record your adventures.
- **The Warrior's Almanac:** a new issue every year, showing in one place what technology was introduced, or went extinct, in the current game year.

#### 6d. Improvements

##### StratCon & Scenarios
*Most of this is covered in the [Contract Overhaul](https://megamek.org/2026/10/20/Feature-Highlight-The-Contract-Overhaul-Project.html) and [Balancing Gameplay & Economics](https://megamek.org/2026/11/07/Feature-Highlight-Balancing-Gameplay-and-Economics.html) highlights.*
- **StratCon overhauled**, with new mechanics and better sector generation.
- **Every scenario template rewritten**, fixing numerous bugs. We also have a working template writer now, so future fixes will be easier to roll out.
- **Advanced Scouting** gets several new sources of equipment bonuses. See the in-game Glossary.

##### Personnel
- **Returning from leave:** characters coming back from leave (maternity leave, for example) go back to their original unit if their seat is still free.
- **The family tree** handles large campaigns better.

##### Medical
- **New advanced surgeries:** permanently fractured bones no longer cripple your command.
- **Broken hips and shoulders** are easier to repair. Broken limbs overall should be far less painful to deal with.

##### Units, Repairs & Logistics
- **Mechanics can maintain infantry:** an option lets mechanics maintain, repair and refit infantry platoons instead of the platoons doing it themselves.
- **Careful salvage:** an option that makes salvaging wrecks take much longer but greatly reduces the risk of accidents.
- **Unused unit bays become cargo space** while travelling.
- **Employer Market** units are delivered instantly.
- **The unit market** now sells small craft.

##### Interface & Performance
- **Memory leaks:** numerous leaks found and fixed.
- **A new "modern" interface style.** We've started experimenting with it. It's far more capable than our old setup and makes things like in-app tutorials possible. Finishing it will take a couple of releases, so please be patient, and constructive feedback is welcome.
- **Glossary:** many new entries, and every entry has been checked for this release.
- ✎ *Also from MegaMek, for MekHQ players: tables remember up to 3 levels of sorting, number formatting is the same across all three apps, and Backspace and the arrow keys work again in MekHQ dialogs.*

##### Setting
- **Dark Age bioweapons** now have a non-canon end date, so the Core Worlds are no longer a permanent plague zone. We doubt canon will ever revisit these bioweapons, but if it does, the dates can be updated to match.

#### 6e. Bug Fixes

##### Personnel
- **The "curse of blandness"** is lifted. All personnel were generating with default attributes and traits.
- **Minimum complement and bulk hiring** now generate characters at the requested skill level.
- We reluctantly fixed the beloved **"doctor baby" bug**. It will be missed.

##### Parts, Repairs & Logistics
- **A massive rules-compliance pass** over all parts, repairs and refits. More improvements are planned for 0.60.00.
- **"Damaged beyond repair"** is no longer applied to parts incorrectly. A part can still end up beyond repair, but only through failed repair checks.
- **Small craft** are no longer ignored by the resupply system.
- **Procurement personnel** no longer waste attempts on impossible tasks.
- **The Parts In Use dialog** no longer forgets your changes.

##### Clans
- **Clan bidding fixed.** Clans were underbidding by a wide margin, which made them easier than intended.
- **The batchall breach warning** no longer loops endlessly until you break the batchall.

#### 6f. Experimental
- **Planetary bases (experimental).** Early in the 0.51.01 cycle we added planetary bases: locations where you can store people, units and parts. It's our first step toward letting you split up your campaign. The developer working on them went on hiatus partway through the cycle, so treat this feature as experimental and expect bugs. Notably, very few of MekHQ's systems have been updated to account for it yet. Another developer has already agreed to finish the system in 0.60.00. ✎ *The Contract Overhaul post calls this "force splitting (experimental)". Use one name in both places.*
- **Support teams.** As part of the new Command Generator, you can now add support personnel to the TO&E. Because of how it works, we expect some minor issues. They should be smaller than the planetary base ones, but don't be surprised if we patch it a couple of times after launch. [Command Generator highlight →](https://megamek.org/2026/11/21/Feature-Highlight-The-Command-Generator.html)
- ✎ *The Contract Overhaul post also labels the StratCon overhaul experimental. Should it be listed here too?*

#### 6g. Missed the Mark
- **Multiple concurrent contracts.** As part of the Contract Overhaul we meant to let players take on several contracts at once. It wasn't finished in time and has been held back until 0.60.00.
- **G.R.E.G.** (the Generic Random Event Generator). We announced in the [0.51.0 Milestone post](https://megamek.org/2026/06/23/New-Milestone-Release-0.51.0.html) that G.R.E.G. would see development in 0.51.01. Some work was done, but the Contract Overhaul took all of the MekHQ team's attention. Work continues in 0.60.00 and probably 0.60.01.
- **Game worlds and tournaments** weren't finished in time. Look for them in 0.60.00.
